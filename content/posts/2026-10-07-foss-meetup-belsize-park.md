There will be a FOSS meetup on Saturday 24th October 2026, at 1pm, at [The
George in Belsize
Park](https://www.greeneking.co.uk/pubs/greater-london/george), called by
Polarian and Matthew Fennell!

**Address**:

250 Haverstock Hill
Belsize Park
Greater London
England
NW3 2AE

The Nearest station is Belsize Park, once leaving the station turn right and
walk straight until you reach the pub.

The next nearest station is Hampstead Heath which is on Mildmay, which might
be faster for some people living in North London. When you leave the station
turn left and walk towards the royal free hospital, turn right until you
reach Hampstead Green walk through Hampstead Green until you come out on the
main road, then turn left and you should see the pub.

[OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/292183282)

**Time**: Saturday 24th October, 1pm onwards.

**RSVP**: not required.

**Finding us on the day**:

Polarian will wear the EuroBSDCon 2026 (Brussels) tshirt ([artwork can be
seen here](https://2026.eurobsdcon.org/)), and will dump some stickers on
the table. You can also look for the [Beastie, the FreeBSD
Mascot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BSD_Daemon). We have not put a
reservation into the venue, as we can't predict the numbers which will turn
up, however the pub is large and has decent beer on tap.
