There will be a FOSS meetup on Friday 27th November 2026, from 7pm until
late, at The Masque Haunt, in Baker Street, called by Polarian on behalf of
FSUG.uk.

**Address**:

168–172 Old Street,
Old Street,
Islington,
EC1V 9BP

Just down the road from Old Street station, see map below:

[OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/294950903)

**Time**: Friday 27th November, from 7pm until late.

**RSVP**: not required.

**Finding us on the day**:

Polarian will wear the EuroBSDCon 2026 (Zagreb) tshirt ([artwork can be seen
here](https://2026.eurobsdcon.org/)), and will dump a FOSS poster and some
stickers on a table nearby. It's not a reserved venue, so we will have to
make do with what space is available on the day, and due to it being after
work, it is likely to be busy.
